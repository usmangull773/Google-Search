Where it breaks, and what I watch for

  • The click ID dropping as the visitor crosses to the booking platform.
  • Consent choices that block the signal before it forms.
  • Field mapping inside the booking platform sending back the wrong thing.
  • Import timing and duplicate conversions inflating the count.

For people who call or leave their number and book on the follow-up, a hashed first-party match brings those in too, so the phone and form path is not left uncounted.

The rule the whole build obeys

Your booking system stays the source of truth. What Google reports is what you can actually see as booked and paid. No phantom conversions, no number you cannot check against your own records.

The plan, in phases with real gates

Start small and be right. Each phase has to earn the next one. No budget jump on a hunch, and no automation switched on to paper over a weak signal.

Out of scope, by design

No Performance Max, no Display, no Shopping, no social. Brand terms stay excluded and added as negatives, so you never pay for the traffic you already earn for free.
